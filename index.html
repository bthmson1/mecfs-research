Exploring the patterns behind ME/CFS

An educational and exploratory research project examining symptoms, exertion, post-exertional malaise, timelines, and recurring experiences reported by people affected by ME/CFS-like illness.

THE PROJECT

Looking for patterns across experiences

ME/CFS can involve a complex combination of neurological, immune, autonomic, metabolic, sleep-related and exertion-related symptoms. This project explores whether recurring patterns can be identified when individual experiences are examined using the same structured questions.

A MAJOR AREA OF INTEREST

Post-exertional malaise

One major focus of this project is understanding what happens before, during and after exertion.

Rather than recording only whether someone experiences PEM, the research can examine timing, triggers, delayed responses, symptom clusters, recovery periods and differences between types of exertion.

Example pattern

1
Activity

Physical, cognitive or emotional exertion.

2
Delay

Symptoms may not immediately increase.

3
Symptom increase

Multiple symptom domains may worsen.

4
Recovery

Return toward an individual's baseline.
