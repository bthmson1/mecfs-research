Reading carefully

A few questions worth keeping nearby.

Who was studied?

Look at sample size, diagnostic criteria, illness severity, age, duration of illness, and how participants were recruited.

What was measured?

Was the study measuring symptoms, blood markers, cognition, activity, autonomic function, imaging, physiology, or something else?

When was it measured?

Timing may matter in PEM research. Measurements before exertion, immediately afterward, and one or more days later may capture different parts of the response.

Was there a comparison group?

A comparison group can help show whether an observation differs from healthy participants or people with another condition.

Has it been repeated?

Findings become more informative when independent researchers observe similar results in other groups.

Association or cause?

Two findings appearing together does not establish that one caused the other.

CAREGIVERS

Caregiver information

ME/CFS can affect partners, family members, friends, and other caregivers as well as the person who is ill.

Public-health and ME/CFS organizations may provide information that can help caregivers better understand the illness and its functional impact.

DAILY LIFE

Practical support

Depending on severity, people may need information related to work, school, disability, mobility, accommodations, caregiving, or activities of daily living.

HEALTHCARE

Preparing for medical appointments

Keeping a concise symptom history, medication list, existing test results, and notes about activity tolerance may make medical visits easier to organize.

Outside educational material can also be discussed with a healthcare professional when appropriate.

Finding care

Looking for a healthcare professional.

Access to clinicians familiar with ME/CFS varies by location.

Patient organizations and clinical education centers may provide directories, educational materials, or suggestions for locating clinicians familiar with ME/CFS, orthostatic intolerance, POTS, Long COVID, or related conditions.

A provider appearing in an outside directory should not be interpreted as an endorsement by this project.

Urgent medical concerns

This website is not an emergency medical service.

The questionnaire and educational material on this website should not be used to obtain urgent medical care.

New, severe, rapidly worsening, or potentially life-threatening symptoms require appropriate medical evaluation.
